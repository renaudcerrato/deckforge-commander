# Changelog

User-facing changes, maintained manually.

## [0.1.1.0](https://github.com/renaudcerrato/deckforge-commander/releases/tag/v0.1.1.0) — 2026-11-02

Initial release of DeckForge Commander.

- **Stream** toggles a live program; **Loop** repeats after completion and a delay; **Run** executes once and retains successful output.
- Displays stdout as text, progress rings or bars, and PNG, JPEG, SVG, or WebP images. Example: `text:42|color:cyan;label:Items`; see the [protocol reference](README.md#protocol).
- Supports macOS 13+ and Windows 11 x64 with Stream Deck 7.1+.
